# Website of Nguyen Phuong Quynh (firsttofile.vn)

Trademark advice for companies registering brands in Vietnam, plus the weekly series First to File.
Owner is not technical: Claude does all technical work and explains each step simply.

- Built with Quarto; published to GitHub Pages by .github/workflows/publish.yml on every push to main.
- Design: beige background, brown text, shades of brown (tokens at the top of styles.scss). Fonts self-hosted in assets/fonts (assets/fonts.css).
- Never show her phone number or personal email on the site.
- She reviews all IP content before it goes live.
- Plain English, no dashes as punctuation, every legal fact traceable to a source.
